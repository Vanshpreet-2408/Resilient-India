⚠️ Disaster Awareness Hub

Common hazards
🌊

Flood

Floods occur when water covers normally dry land. Technology can support forecasting, monitoring and early warnings.

🌍

Earthquake

Seismic monitoring, hazard mapping and communication systems support earthquake risk reduction.

🌀

Cyclone

Satellites, weather forecasting and early warning systems help communities prepare.

⛰️

Landslide

Remote sensing, GIS and rainfall monitoring can support landslide risk assessment.

☀️

Heat Wave

Weather forecasts and public alerts can help communities prepare for extreme heat.

🔥

Fire

Detection systems, satellite monitoring and emergency communication can support fire management.

🛰️ Technology for Disaster Risk Reduction

Technology + People = Resilience
📱 Mobile Alerts

Mobile phones can deliver warnings and important information quickly.

🚨 Early Warning Systems

Warnings can give people time to prepare and respond.

🛰️ Satellite Technology

Satellites provide useful information for monitoring weather and hazards.

📍 GPS & GIS

GPS identifies locations while GIS supports hazard mapping and spatial analysis.

🌦️ Weather Forecasting

Forecasts support preparation for rainfall, cyclones and heat waves.

🤖 AI & Data Analysis

AI and data analysis can help identify patterns and support risk planning.
